# Tutorial 7 Phylogenetic reconstruction and robustness
MBLS207 – Bioinformatics

Phylogenetic trees are an excellent way to characterise the evolutionary history of groups of homologous sequences. They can serve a multitude of purposes to help us understand the past and, sometimes, elaborate adequate predictions about the future. In this tutorial, you will be confronted with various applications of phylogenetic trees, with the main aim to interpret them correctly, and shed lights on a diverse set of biological problems
