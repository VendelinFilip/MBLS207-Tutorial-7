
EXERCISE 2: BONE MORPHOGENETIC PROTEINS

Bone Morphogenetic Proteins (BMPs) are signalling proteins in humans. As the name suggests, they are involved in bone formation, and therefore important for the building of our skeleton. In medicine, one particular BMP, called BMP2, has been shown to effectively help grow bones and achieve spinal fusions.

Recently, a BMP2 homolog (BMP2/4) was found in the calcifying (=exoskeleton building) coral *Acropora digitifera*. Since coral skeletons have some similarities to bones it would be interesting to find out whether the homolog of human BMP2, which is involved in building bones in humans, is also involved in building the exoskeleton in *Acropora digitifera*.
